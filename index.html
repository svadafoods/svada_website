At Svada, every pop is made with ingredients you'd proudly recognize: fresh milk, real fruit pulp, and pure Coorg chocolate sourced from Karnataka. No fillers or artificial essences. Our vibrant colors come from plant-based ingredients, including spirulina, the nutrient rich superfood naturally rich in iron, zinc, and protein along with premium natural food colors. We keep every recipe clean with no preservatives, no added sugar, no synthetic dyes, and no unnecessary artificial additives.

The result is a pop that's crafted for flavor, not shortcuts. With only 6–10% fat (compared to around 30% in conventional ice cream) and just 20% air overrun (instead of nearly 50%), every bite is denser, creamier, and packed with real taste. Served at −6°C, our pops are intentionally warmer than traditional ice cream, allowing the natural fruit and chocolate flavors to shine from the very first bite.

HOW WE MAKE

From orchard
to your bite.

01

Source the Chocolate

We import pure, single origin chocolate from Coorg, Karnataka, No additives, no blending with inferior grades.

02

Melt and Colour the Shell

Chocolate is gently melted and blended with 100% plant based colours.

03

Pour into Fruit Moulds

The chocolate is poured into custom fruit shaped moulds.

04

Set and Remove Excess

Once the shell sets, we tip and rotate the moulds to pour out excess chocolate, leaving a perfect thin, even shell.

05

Fill with Gelato and Seal

Shells are filled with slow churned gelato made from fresh milk and real fruit pulp. A final layer of chocolate seals the mould.

06

Frozen and Packed

Each completed Mixed Pop is snap frozen to lock in peak freshness and flavour. No compromises, pure joyful bites.
